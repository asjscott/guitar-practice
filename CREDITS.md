# Credits

Third-party assets used by GuitarPractice. Add an entry whenever an asset is added
(sound packs, fonts, icons), with its source URL, licence and required attribution.

None of them is committed: `scripts/fetch-sounds` fetches what `sounds.json` lists into the git-ignored `Sounds/`.
